# V6.2 — panel over a full-screen app, and across Spaces

**Phase:** 6   **Needs:** a human at the machine, both TCC grants, at least two Spaces and one
full-screen app.
**Discharges:** the `assumption` at P0.1 and across Phase 3 — the panel's `collectionBehavior`
flags are prior art, not a measurement (D13); and the P2.4 `assumption` — a window genuinely on a
second Space reporting a different `SpaceID`.

## Goal

Confirm by screenshot (not by the absence of an error) that the switcher panel:

1. appears **over** a macOS full-screen app rather than behind it or on the desktop Space, and
2. follows the user when they switch Spaces — summon on Space 1, switch to Space 2, summon again,
   panel is there too.

And, while set up for it, confirm P2.4's converse: a window really on Space 2 is reported with a
`SpaceID` different from Space 1's.

## Prerequisites

- Accessibility **and** Screen Recording granted to `build/GoTab.app` (build with
  `./scripts/build.sh`, run the bundle so TCC identity is stable — `gotab -check` reports both).
- Two Spaces (Mission Control → add a desktop), and one app in native full-screen (e.g. Safari,
  ⌃⌘F).
- A configured hotkey (default ⌥⇥).

## Protocol

1. `open build/GoTab.app` (or run it installed). Confirm `-check` shows both grants.
2. **Full-screen:** enter a full-screen app. Press ⌥⇥. Screenshot (⌘⇧5). The panel must be visible
   and centred on that display, on top of the full-screen window.
3. **Across Spaces:** on Space 1, ⌥⇥, screenshot. Switch to Space 2 (⌃→). ⌥⇥, screenshot. Panel
   present both times, on the display under the pointer.
4. **SpaceID converse:** put one window on Space 1 and one on Space 2. Run `gotab -list` (or `-watch`)
   from a terminal and read the Space column — the two windows carry different non-zero `SpaceID`s.
   With `ShowOtherSpace=false` (the default), summon on Space 1 and confirm the Space-2 window is
   filtered out; flip it with `gotab -prefs ShowOtherSpace=true` and confirm it returns.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Screenshot: panel over a full-screen app.
- [ ] Screenshots: panel on Space 1 and on Space 2 across one switch.
- [ ] `gotab -list` shows two windows with distinct non-zero `SpaceID`s, and `ShowOtherSpace`
      filters/unfilters the off-Space one as expected.
- [ ] Findings + the SpaceID numbers appended to `docs/DECISIONS.md`; `docs/ROADMAP.md` Phase 6 row
      → ✅, P0.1's open half closed, the Phase 3 and P2.4 `assumption` tags marked discharged.

## If the panel is behind the full-screen app or does not follow Spaces

That is a `panel.m` `collectionBehavior` change (`canJoinAllSpaces` /
`fullScreenAuxiliary` / `stationary`), and it is exactly the failure mode switchers most often hit —
send it back to P3.1 with the screenshot, do not paper over it.
